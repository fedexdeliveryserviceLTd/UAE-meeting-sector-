Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

UAE Meeting Sector is an independent private meeting-request portal. It is not an official UAE or Dubai government website and is not affiliated with a government department.

Information we collect

When you create an account or submit a request, we may collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), organisation (if provided), meeting purpose, preferred date and time, time zone, account identifier, request status and any administrator note relating to your request.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To receive, review and manage meeting requests.
  • To show you the status and history of your requests.
  • To send administrative notifications and decision emails about a request.
  • To protect the portal, troubleshoot errors and prevent misuse.

Service providers

The portal is hosted using GitHub Pages. Account and request information is processed using Supabase. Email notifications are sent through Google services configured for this portal. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy notices.

Sharing and retention

We do not offer your personal information for sale. Request details may be accessible to authorised portal administrators and the service providers described above. Information is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, manage requests, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion; we may need to verify your identity and may retain information where there is a legitimate reason to do so.

Security

We use account sign-in and access controls intended to protect information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not submit passport numbers, payment-card details, passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information through this portal.

Children and changes

This service is intended for adults acting for themselves or their organisation. We may update this notice as the service changes; the latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email uaesectorsupport@gmail.com.

Important: This is a practical privacy notice, not a legal compliance certification. The operator should obtain appropriate legal review for the countries where the service is operated and used.
